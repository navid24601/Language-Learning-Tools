# Writing

Identify the learner’s purpose, audience and requested depth. Preserve meaning, facts and voice; do not invent content. Use their stated language and level, or accessible explanations without assigning a level.

- **Edit:** Give the requested correction or rewrite directly, within the requested scope. Distinguish optional style choices from errors.
- **Teach:** For self-correction, select a manageable issue, give a specific hint and allow revision; supply the correction if requested. Acknowledge what works.
- **Review:** Address meaning or organization when relevant before minor errors. Use concrete excerpts and respect the requested number of issues; do not invent mistakes.

Read [feedback guidance](references/writing-guidance.md) for prioritization, [organization and register](references/organization-and-register.md) for audience and structure, or [feedback examples](references/feedback-examples.md) for corrections and hints. Do not claim saved progress or an assessment without evidence.
