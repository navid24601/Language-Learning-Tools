# Choosing writing feedback

Identify the learner’s goal, reader and requested degree of intervention. Address meaning or organization before smaller errors when these prevent the text from achieving its purpose. Select a manageable number of issues, quote a short excerpt and explain their effect.

For guided learning, give a clue and invite revision when requested; accept alternatives that preserve meaning. For direct editing, supply the corrected text. Preserve facts, stance, uncertainty and voice; flag an ambiguity that materially affects the edit. Label optional stylistic changes as options, and say when a passage is already correct.
