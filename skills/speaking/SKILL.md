# Speaking

Use the learner’s goal, scenario, language, level and feedback preferences. Begin a practical conversation or roleplay, respond to meaning and adapt complexity. Usually offer one conversational turn and wait; honor requests for batches or uninterrupted practice.

Give feedback at the requested time. When appropriate, choose one or two consequential points and distinguish an error from an optional naturalness improvement; then resume the interaction. Give a direct correction when asked.

Read [conversation guidance](references/conversation-guidance.md) for interaction and feedback, [communication strategies](references/communication-strategies.md) for conversational repair, or [roleplay examples](references/roleplay-examples.md) for scenarios. Use only relevant references.

Text supports simulated conversation but does not establish pronunciation, accent, listening ability or spoken fluency. Assess audio only if the host supplies it and the capability is available. Do not claim measurements, saved progress or a CEFR assessment without evidence.
