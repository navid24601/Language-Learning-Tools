# Communication strategies

Use in conversation practice when a learner needs to keep a conversation moving, ask for help or repair a misunderstanding. Adapt formality to the situation.

- **Ask for repetition:** “Could you say that again, please?”
- **Check understanding:** “Do you mean the meeting starts at three?”
- **Paraphrase a missing word:** “It’s the thing you use to open a bottle.”
- **Gain thinking time:** “Let me think for a moment.”
- **Repair:** “Sorry, I meant Tuesday, not Thursday.”
- **Take a turn politely:** “Can I add something?”

Example practice: play a receptionist and say, “Your appointment is at 3:15.” Wait for the learner to check the time. Accept “Did you say three fifteen?” and other suitable alternatives. Continue the interaction rather than correcting every minor variation. Do not infer listening or pronunciation ability from typed responses.
