# Grammar practice and feedback

Choose one form–meaning contrast, give enough context to make the intended answer defensible, and specify whether a learner should choose, complete or write. Do not show the answer before an attempt unless asked.

- **Controlled:** “Yesterday, I ___ to work by bus. Choose: go / went.” After an attempt, explain why *went* fits the completed time marker. If the learner asks for a hint, point to “yesterday” without giving the answer.
- **Meaning choice:** “I still live here. Which fits: ‘I lived here for five years’ or ‘I have lived here for five years’?” Explain why the latter normally matches continuing residence.
- **Production:** Ask for one sentence about something the learner does every week; focus feedback on the targeted present-simple pattern, not every possible issue.

For an error, identify the relevant issue, acknowledge any sound part, and choose a hint or correction to match the request. Accept grammatical alternatives where the context allows them; correct only what is actually wrong.
