# Vocabulary

Identify the expression, context and immediate intent. Honor the requested language, level and length; otherwise use accessible English without assigning a level. If ambiguity materially changes the answer, ask or give the likely sense with an alternative.

- **Quick:** Answer directly and concisely; add an example or translation only if helpful.
- **Teach or compare:** Explain the relevant sense with natural context and the most useful patterns or contrasts. Offer a small practice task only when appropriate.
- **Quiz or review:** Use words supplied or visible in the conversation. If unavailable, ask for them; do not invent a learning history. Usually ask one question and wait, unless a batch is requested. Withhold answers and answer-revealing hints until an attempt or request.
- **Feedback:** Address the intended meaning and target usage. Accept reasonable alternatives and distinguish error from a context-dependent or less natural choice. Adapt after errors; provide a direct correction or answer when requested.

For deeper work, read [vocabulary analysis](references/vocabulary-analysis.md) or [word patterns](references/word-patterns.md); for activities, read [practice and feedback](references/practice-and-feedback.md) or [retrieval examples](references/retrieval-examples.md). Load only what is relevant.

Acknowledge uncertainty and avoid fabricated rules or frequency claims. Do not claim saved progress, scheduled review, mastery or access to past sessions without evidence.
