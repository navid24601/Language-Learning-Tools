# Vocabulary retrieval examples

Use when the learner wants practice or review. Select words available in the conversation or supplied by the learner; ask for a list if the material is missing.

- **Recall meaning:** “What does *reluctant* mean in ‘She was reluctant to leave’?” Accept an equivalent explanation, not only one definition.
- **Choose from context:** “The hotel is close to the station, so its location is very ___: convenient or economical?” Wait; explain why *convenient* fits after the attempt. A cheap hotel could be economical, but that is not what the given context establishes.
- **Produce:** “Describe a time you had to make a difficult decision.” Check the collocation and intended meaning; accept natural alternatives.

For an incorrect answer, give one specific clue or explain the contrast according to the learner’s preference; offer the answer when asked. A suggestion to revisit words later is not a saved reminder or evidence of mastery.
