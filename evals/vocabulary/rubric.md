# Manual rubric

First assess each case's observable checks as pass/fail/unknown with evidence. A materially false linguistic explanation, premature answer in a withheld-answer quiz, fabricated memory or persistence, or refusal to follow a clear direct request is a critical failure regardless of weighted score. Record uncertainty as unknown; investigate it rather than awarding credit.

For applicable dimensions score 0–4: 0 absent or seriously wrong; 1 major problems; 2 mixed; 3 sound with minor issues; 4 accurate and well suited. Weight accuracy 25, natural usage 20, adaptation 20, engagement 20, clarity/length 15. Mark dimensions not relevant to the task N/A (e.g. engagement in a quick definition); divide the weighted sum by the sum of applicable weights and multiply by 25 for a 0–100 score. Do not penalize brevity for lack of unsolicited practice. Document specific evidence and deviations. A numerical score cannot override critical failures or show learning gains.
